PostPilot — BY GAATHA is a unified social media management, automation, and campaign scheduling application designed to help businesses, creators, and organizations manage multiple social accounts from a single command center.

What is PostPilot?

PostPilot is a centralized social media management system engineered to streamline content planning, visual validation, and scheduled publishing across diverse social networks. By unifying destination assets under an intuitive multi-tenant structure, PostPilot allows marketing teams and independent operators to maintain a consistent digital presence without switching between fragmented platform interfaces.

Created under the identity PostPilot — BY GAATHA, the platform is crafted with a focus on operational safety, visual precision, and predictable execution. Every feature is architected to eliminate accidental dispatches, maintain strict token isolation, and give users full visibility into their publishing pipeline.

Core Capabilities & Workflows

PostPilot brings together the vital elements of enterprise social media operations into one cohesive workflow:

Unified Multi-Account Management

Connect and organize multiple social accounts across different brands and organizational tiers, including Meta verified Facebook Pages, linked Instagram accounts, independent LinkedIn applications, and verified WhatsApp Business accounts.

Guided Content Creation & Previews

Author social posts with real-time character counters, hashtag categorization, and high-fidelity live mockups that mirror Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn feed presentations before any post is queued.

Precision Multi-Timezone Scheduling

Coordinate release schedules across global audiences with granular timezone management, including default support for Asia/Kolkata (IST), alongside interactive Month, Week, Day, and List calendar views.

Engineered Publishing Safeguards

Built with a defense-in-depth safety engine where publishing is explicitly gated by environment controls (POSTPILOT_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=0 by default), preventing accidental live dispatches during rehearsals or development.

Drafts & Approval Workspaces

Manage campaign concepts through structured draft lifecycles, revision tracking, platform guideline validations, and formal team review stages before converting them into actionable publishing events.

Lifecycle Diagnostics & Audit Trail

Inspect exact job histories, worker execution logs, retry exhaustion tracking, and API diagnostic payloads, ensuring accountability and fast troubleshooting when third-party endpoints encounter network turbulence.
